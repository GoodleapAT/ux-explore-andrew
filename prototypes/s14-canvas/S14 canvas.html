One customer, twenty six days, fourteen frames

Carla Brenner calls Brightline Heating and Air at 7:50am on Tuesday 14 July with no cooling in a heat wave. She is a brand new customer. Twenty six days later the county green tags the job and it completes itself. Read the strip left to right.

Frames 01 and 02 are new and are what this round is testing: what a CSR actually does in the first ninety seconds of a call. Frames 03 to 14 are memo 7 drawn as a sequence. Data is the S14 worked example. Nothing here is invented.

The three questions this is for

  1. Does anything tell anyone the container exists? It is created at 7:52am on frame 03, it holds nothing until frame 08, and no frame mentions it. Frames 05, 08 and 14 are where that bites.
  2. Can four status levels be hidden? S14 has one interest, so no frame shows more than two.
  3. Does the strip read as one journey without the memo open.

What is not here

The kitchen table proposal, the payment promise and the two night stall are out of scope this round. The labelled block between frames 07 and 08 holds their place. Frames 06 and 11 are phone surfaces, placeholdered at phone size.

Conventions

[i] marks annotation inside a frame and is never interface copy. Labels above and notes below a frame are canvas furniture, not screen copy. No reference codes anywhere. Colour appears where somebody has to act, once per frame at most.
