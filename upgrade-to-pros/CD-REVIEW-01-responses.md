## The four things that need your ruling

Everything else below is routine routing. These four are not.

### 1 · Your lead-tapped comment contradicts the current model

You wrote, on a lead row in the S15 customer record:

> I am imaging a sheet that opens with lead information, and a CTA to create a project to pursue the
> lead.

**There is no longer anything to create.** Under T-2 the project exists from the moment the lead was
created, which is the moment it was qualified. A call to action that creates one is the old
pursuit-mints model, which held until the afternoon of the same day.

**Three ways out, and they are genuinely different products.**

| | What the call to action does | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Rename it** | "Start selling", and it moves the lead from qualified to being actively worked. The project already exists and is not mentioned | The container stays invisible, consistent with the canvas finding |
| **Keep create, move the mint** | The project is created here rather than at qualification. **That contradicts T-2**, which is the team's position and not ours to move | We would be designing against a position we have rejected |
| **Drop the call to action** | Tapping a lead opens a sheet that reads. Selling begins by writing a proposal, not by pressing anything | Simplest, and it loses the moment you were trying to design |

**The first is what I would take**, and it keeps your bundling follow-on intact: pressing it can still
ask whether to include other open leads.

### 2 · CD answered the to-decide, and the answer is about the model

**The container cannot be mentioned on any of the three frames where it bites**, and CD gave three
separate reasons rather than one.

Frame 05: four minutes old, one interest, one appointment, nothing the lead does not say better.
Frame 08: holds a signed contract and a job, but the page is a work page with a stage rail, and **a
second spine would compete with the one actually driving the screen.** Frame 14: its only distinctive
content is two money cards that cannot be added together.

**Memo 7's to-decide card says that if this is the answer, the model is wrong rather than the
layout.** It is the answer. So the finding is: **a container is created two minutes into a phone call
and no surface can justify naming it for the first two days of its life.**

**What that does not settle.** Whether the container is wrong, or whether it is correctly a backend
fact with no early surface. Those are different conclusions and the canvas cannot tell them apart.

### 3 · The four-status test did not run

No frame shows more than two status levels, and CD says plainly that this proves nothing: **S14 has
one interest, so the interest and the lead are the same thing from every angle**, and the container is
never drawn.

**The Thompson case is where four grains actually collide.** Three interests on one lead, one
deferred. Until that is drawn, the biggest risk we logged is untested.

### 4 · The customer record sections

You wrote:

> This is where having 'Sales' as the section name is not helpful. I would make all sales focused on
> leads and projects (but only projects that are being pursued). [...] So instead of a 'Jobs' under
> 'Operations', there is an 'Active projects' and 'Jobs' are nested in each project.

**This reverses a decision of 13 September**, which put jobs flat under an Active projects heading
precisely so that nothing indents on the customer record, on the grounds that three leads collapsing
into two committed scopes has to stay representable.

**And it collides with finding 2 above.** Nesting jobs inside projects makes the container the
organising device on the customer record, at the same moment the canvas concluded the container cannot
be named anywhere. Both cannot be right.

**Worth knowing before you rule:** the 13 September decision already records itself as provisional and
says a project changing section when a contract is signed means two places to look. **Your comment is
the other half of that problem**, so this is a real question rather than a reversal for its own sake.

## How many screens the customer page request actually is

You asked for the customer page after qualification, and separately for it **after every beat that
follows**. On S14 that is nine or ten beats.

**It is not nine or ten screens. It is one page in nine states**, and the customer record already has
a component record. **So the useful output is what changes beat to beat and what stays still**, which
is a much smaller drawing than nine pages and a much better test.

**And it is the right place to have asked for it.** The customer page is the one surface where the
lead and the project are both visible or both hidden, because it is the anchor they hang off rather
than the work itself. **If the container never needs naming there, it probably never needs naming
anywhere**, which is the strongest available test of finding 2.
