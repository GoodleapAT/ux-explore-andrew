| 4 | **Does a Home App contact auto-qualify into a lead?** | **Yes**, and so does any contact naming a trade and a property. **So the capture screen is a lead screen for most contacts.** An inquiry screen is still needed for the contact that names neither |
| 5 | **What is qualification testing, intent or completeness?** | **Intent**, defined as a trade and a property being named. **The definition is C's reading rather than Andrew's ruling and wants confirming** |

**What is blocking now is different and smaller.** Confirming the qualification test, and three
questions the rulings created: what closes a lead, whether the container's name may shrink, and
whether qualification is a gate or something you undo. **All four are in the open items.**

## Learned from

- **15 Sep 2026.** Sorting a review queue by whether a comment changes a claim or changes an
  appearance, before answering any of it. **Half of a twenty four comment queue turned out to be
  visual**, which a design memo cannot carry and which would have been quietly dropped. Worth doing
  on every queue from now on.
- **15 Sep 2026.** One comment was overtaken by a model change made the day before it was written, and
  a second was already half done. **A queue read cold, without checking it against the decision log,
  would have drawn a rejected model on Andrew's own instruction.**
