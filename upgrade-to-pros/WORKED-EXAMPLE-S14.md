# The worked example for S14

**The Brenners. Sell and install with the money off-system, and a customer who has never bought
anything before.**

> **Who may write to this file:** any surface that can write. Edit in place. **Do not invent a value
> that belongs here without adding it here as well**, or the next artefact will invent a different
> one.
>
> **This is a second data set, not a second example.** The Thompsons in `WORKED-EXAMPLE.md` remain the
> canonical multi-trade case. S14 needed its own because it is a **different organisation, a
> different trade and a brand new customer**, and none of the Thompson data survives that. Decided
> 14 Sep 2026, when the standard's one-example rule was read as being about not re-inventing the same
> customer per artefact rather than about never having a second scenario.
>
> **Nouns per `VOCABULARY.md`. The demand chain and the mint point are T-3 and T-2**, in
> `TEAM-POSITIONS.md`, and are not restated here.

## Provenance, and what C refined

The narrative is the team's scenario **S14, "Sell + install, money off-system"**, in Confluence,
authored by Joel Feyereisen and adopted 1 September 2026. **Built from the What happens and Notes
columns only.** The Entities column on that page is generated rather than authored, so it is a
reading of DRAFT v3 and not a specification; nothing below is taken from it.

**Four things C had to settle to make the narrative internally consistent. All four want Andrew's
ruling.**

1. **The rebate does not match the option they bought.** Beat 15 files a **utility heat-pump rebate**.
   The option chosen at beat 7 is **Better**, a two-stage condenser with a new pad and a surge
   protector. The heat pump is **Best**, which they declined. **Resolved here as a high-efficiency
   air-conditioning rebate**, which a two-stage condenser plausibly qualifies for, because the
   alternative is to change which option they bought and that would break beats 4, 5, 7 and the whole
   money promise. **Flagged: the script may simply have the wrong rebate name.**
2. **"We'll decide tomorrow" and they decide on Thursday.** Beat 6 is Tuesday evening and beat 7 is
   Thursday 8am. Kept as written, because a customer saying tomorrow and taking two days is truer than
   either alternative, and it makes the stall in beat 6 last two nights rather than one. **The extra
   day is deliberate, not an error.**
3. **The tender promise differs from its own twin.** S14 beat 5 is half by cheque at the start and
   half at completion. The S14B header describes $500 deposit, half on install day, and the balance at
   inspection-complete. **S14's own wording is used here**, since S14B is a separate page and not in
   scope.
4. **No dates, names or figures existed.** The narrative gives weekdays, times and three prices and
   nothing else. Everything below that is not in that list was authored here, which is the point of
   this file.

**Dates run past today on purpose.** This is a designed narrative that has to reach a close-out.

## The contractor

**Brightline Heating and Air.** Twelve technicians, replacement and service, Fresno. Two comfort
advisors selling, one production coordinator, four install crews of two.

**Product tier: payments and selling, with operations. Money off-platform.** The advisors sell
on-platform. Production moved on-platform when the office whiteboard died: scheduling, purchase
orders, permits, checklists, the equipment registry. **Money never moved.** It stays in QuickBooks
and paper cheques.

**This is the scenario's whole point and it is not a tier.** Brightline has the money capability and
does not use it, which is different from not having it. Anything drawn for S14 has to look right for
an organisation that has switched a capability off rather than one that was never sold it.

## Demand: one inquiry, one opportunity

**7:50am, Tuesday 14 July 2026, mid-heat-wave.** Carla Brenner calls: *"Our AC died overnight. How
soon can someone look at it?"*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inquiry** | Raised Tue 14 Jul, 7:50am. Route: inbound telephone call, taken by Dana Whitlock |
| **What Carla gave** | Name, cell number, address. **And the answer to one question: the system is about seventeen years old** |
| **The triage** | Seventeen years and R-22 means replacement rather than repair. **Dana makes that call on the phone**, and it is what sends this to an advisor instead of a service technician |
| **Qualified** | Tue 14 Jul, **7:50am, automatically.** The call names a trade and a property, so intent is expressed and the lead exists from the moment Dana writes both down. **Nobody marks anything** |
| **Opportunity** | One. HVAC replacement. **Estimate unknown at qualification** and stays unknown until the kitchen table |

**Corrected 15 September 2026.** This table said Qualified at 7:52am **by Dana Whitlock**, two
minutes after the call connected, as a deliberate act. **Qualification is now automatic on expressed
intent**, and Carla gives a trade and an address in the first sentence. **So the qualification is the
capture, not a step after it**, and the two minute gap and the attribution both go.

**What Dana still does at 7:52 is the triage**, deciding that seventeen years and R-22 means an
advisor rather than a technician. **That was always a separate judgement from qualification** and
this correction makes the separation visible rather than creating it. Where the triage lives is still
open.

**One opportunity, and that is the other reason this scenario is useful.** The Thompsons have three. The
Brenners have one, so every place the interface distinguishes a lead from what is on it has to survive
the case where the distinction shows nothing.

**Status through the scenario.** The inquiry reads Qualified at 7:50, with no time spent as New. The
opportunity reads On a lead from 7:50, Quoted from Tuesday
4:40pm, and Won on Thursday 8:04am. The lead reads Active from 7:50 and **closes at the hand-off on Thursday 16 July**, having nothing
left on it. The project runs on to the close without it.

**And this is the sharpest contrast with S15 in the whole pair.** Here qualification and pursuit are
**two minutes** apart. In S15 they are **six days** apart, with a hand-off from the front desk to the
sales consultant in between. **Same two acts, same two objects, two orders of magnitude between
them**, and one component set has to carry both. **It was three orders of magnitude until 15
September**, when the front of S15 was compressed.

## What is deliberately not specified

Do not invent these, and say so if a screen needs one.

- **Anything about the Brenners before 14 July 2026.** They are new. That is the whole point
- **An email address for Carla** before the proposal is sent. Dana took a cell number
- **Photo and document contents.** Counts and captions only
- **Labour hours per person.** Labour is a total
