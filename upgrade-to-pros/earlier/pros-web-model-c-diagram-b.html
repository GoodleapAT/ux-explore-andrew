The project, selling phase through operations phase

Opportunities track potential work. A project is created to propose against one or more of them, and starts as little more than a place to prepare that proposal. Winning turns it into somewhere work and money are managed, and pushes new statuses back onto the opportunities.

Two phases in one project, with what each phase actually holds

The project starts thin, and now you can see it

The selling phase holds property details, notes and a proposal. That is all. Listing it is what proves the project is a workspace rather than a document, which until now the diagram asserted in a sentence and never showed.

Won opportunities recede

The deferred one leads, at full weight, because it is the only one still worth pursuing. The two that were won sit below a line saying they are archived but kept for context. Attention follows what is still open rather than what already closed.

The boundary test is generous, not strict

Most change rides on the existing master invoice at no price change, or fits the finance headroom, or takes a cash split. Only genuinely separate work becomes a new project, which is a much wider door than an earlier draft of this memo implied.
