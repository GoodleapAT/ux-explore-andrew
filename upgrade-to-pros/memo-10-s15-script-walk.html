Upgrade to ProsS15, the script walkedMockup15 September 2026

Fourteen screens against twenty one beats, with the money worked out across the whole run

This walks the rewritten S15 script beat by beat and draws only the beats its UX Specific column asks for. The beat and its instruction sit beside each mock so the two can be read against each other. Six beats are deliberately not drawn and they are listed before the sections start.

The money was not specified and had to be worked out. The script gives a total at beat 7, a deposit and a plan at beat 8, an instruction to use judgement at beat 11, a balance that updates at 19, an invoice raised at 20 and a milestone paid at 21. Read across those, and the money has five readings on one card, set out in the second panel below.

Settled, for the purposes of this memo

Read off the script. None of it is ratified by anybody but Andrew.

  • Two trades, both sold, two jobs. Roofing $38,900 and siding $34,200, $73,100 together. Both delivered in the same week by different crews.
  • The call creates everything. Sara telephones, the call names a trade and a property, and a lead and a project exist before Renata hangs up.
  • $500 deposit, $72,600 on completion. Two milestones, not three.
  • Nothing in this script is ever blocked and nobody has to act. No change order, no HOA step. The permits are the only dependency and they are issued without incident.
  • Final payment is by credit card, per beat 21. The deposit was a cheque. Two tender instruments on one plan, which the script does not comment on.

The money, worked out across the script

Five readings of one card. Beat 11's instruction was to use judgement.

  • Beat 1, at the customer. Nothing owed. A closed loan that was never Northgate's receivable, and a plan billing $29 a month.
  • Beat 11, the plan exists and one milestone is paid. Contract $73,100, collected $500, outstanding $72,600, not yet due.
  • Beat 19, completion is the trigger, so the balance becomes due. Same figures, different reading. Nothing has been invoiced yet, which is a real state and the script implies it by separating 19 from 20.
  • Beat 20, the invoice is raised for the milestone, pre-filled at $72,600.
  • Beat 21, paid by card. Outstanding $0 and the project closes.

The reading this rests on: the payment plan lives at the project from the contract and carries both milestones, and an invoice is raised per milestone when its trigger fires. So "create invoice" at beat 20 raises the second one rather than creating the first thing money has ever lived in. Say if that is wrong, because five screens rest on it.
