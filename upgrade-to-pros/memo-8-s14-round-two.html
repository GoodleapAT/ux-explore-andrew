S14 round two: the first ninety seconds, and the page where the container finally earns its name

Memo 7 walked all twenty beats. The canvas drew fourteen frames from it. This memo carries every screen, so it is one input rather than a diff: the nine that changed are drawn, and the five you did not comment on are carried at the end with what stands and why. Rebuilding a canvas from a diff means holding two memos and a comment file at once, which is the context loss this arrangement exists to prevent.

Three things landed while writing it. Start selling is the act that moves interests from new to pursuing. The container becomes visible at the hand-off. And one or more trades are selected at the inquiry, which drives the inquiry questions, the site assessment and the hand-off from a single choice.

The trade selection turned out to be the most consequential of the three, so the first three sections are one screen at three moments: blank, the trade resolving, and the trade-typed form being worked. The transformation is the argument, and drawing only the end state hides it.

Annotation inside a mockup is prefixed [i]. It does not travel to the canvas; the argument stays in this memo. Data is from the S14 worked example. Editorial, not interactive.

Settled, for this memo

Five of these are new since memo 7 and all five are yours

  • One or more trades are selected at the inquiry. That selection makes the inquiry questions available, makes the site assessment templates available, and affects the hand-off. One choice, three consequences.
  • The inquiry and the assessment may be one continued capture rather than two forms, so shared fields are not entered twice and the advisor is not re-asking what the CSR already took down.
  • A qualified inquiry is a lead, and a lead has a project from that moment. During selling the two are coextensive, so the distinction has no visible content until a second lead or a job arrives. Calling that page the project page is correct.
  • The container becomes visible at the hand-off and not before. The customer record lists leads only under selling, then groups by project with jobs nested once work exists.
  • Start selling moves interests from new to pursuing. It creates nothing. The bundling question it asks is which interests, not which other leads.
  • Matching is live and continuous, not a separate act before capture.
  • Qualifying leads into booking. One flow, not two screens with a gap.

To decide, before anything gets built

A model question that falls straight out of the template answer

  • Do the trades chosen at the inquiry become the interests? The vocabulary says an interest is one trade inside a lead. If trades are chosen at the inquiry they exist before the lead does, so either the inquiry holds proto-interests or the choice is a form setting that becomes interests at qualification.
  • It matters because of what happens when nothing is qualified. If they are interests, a disqualified inquiry leaves dead interests behind and something has to say so. If they are a form setting, the trades vanish with the inquiry and what a customer once asked about has no answer.
  • And because of the assessment merge. A capture continued from the inquiry into the assessment only works if the thing being continued is one object. Two forms sharing a name is a weaker claim than one capture in two sittings.
  • No screen here can settle it. Sections A to C are drawn assuming a form setting, because it is the weaker claim and the one that survives being wrong.

Everything else is arguable without it. Sections A, C and H are where it would change something.

Why one or more, and not one

What is deliberately absent

No empty questions, no placeholder form, no greyed section saying questions will appear. The screen shows nothing about the trade because no trade has been chosen, and drawing the shape of what is coming would be explaining the interface to the person using it.
